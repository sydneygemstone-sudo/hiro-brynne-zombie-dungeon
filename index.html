Classroom Game · V2.1

Zombie Dungeon

Explore the dungeon, find the crypt key, unlock the forbidden chamber, defeat the Zombie King, claim the treasure, then make it back to the entrance alive.

🗝️ Find the key 🚪 Unlock the crypt 👑 Defeat the boss 🏆 Escape with treasure

Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, drag mouse to look, Space or F to attack, E to heal.
iPad: left-side joystick, right-side swipe to look, on-screen attack and heal buttons.
▶ Play Single Player

This web version runs entirely in your browser. The project also keeps the original LAN co-op server for classroom two-player sessions.
